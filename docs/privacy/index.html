Privacy Policy

Mr. Lister (Mister Lister) by Silver Spectre Software
Effective date: 2 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mr. Lister (“the App”) handles information. The App is designed to work offline. We do not require an account and we do not operate a Mr. Lister cloud backend that receives your shopping lists.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores data locally on your device, which may include:

  • Shopping lists, item quantities, notes, and completion status
  • App settings (for example theme, reminder preferences, Pro mascot preference)
  • Predictor habit history derived from completed shopping trips (kept even if you delete a completed list)
  • Achievement progress and related local state
  • Optional home-screen widget snapshots of an active list

This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it (see below).

2. Sharing and import

If you share a list, the App can create a share card, QR code, or link that contains the list content you chose to share. Anyone who receives that share can view the shared list content. Opening an import link uses a public redirect page only to hand the payload to the App on your device.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may schedule local reminders on your device (for example unfinished lists or Predictor suggestions). These reminders are generated on-device; we do not send them from a Mr. Lister server.

4. Purchases (Pro)

Optional Pro features are sold through the platform store (for example Google Play Billing). Purchase processing, payment details, and related account data are handled by the store provider under their own policies. The App receives purchase status needed to unlock Pro features.

5. Permissions

Depending on your device and settings, the App may request permission to:

  • Show notifications
  • Run after device reboot so scheduled local reminders can be restored

6. Children

The App is a general grocery utility and is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under the age required by applicable law in your region.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local App data remains until you clear App storage, uninstall the App, or delete items/lists inside the App. Uninstalling removes on-device App data. Predictor history is local only.

8. Third-party services

Platform providers (Google Play / device OS) may process technical information as part of installing, updating, billing, or operating the App. Grocery icons may include OpenMoji assets (CC BY-SA 4.0); that does not involve sending your list data to OpenMoji.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Silver Spectre Software through the developer contact email on the Google Play listing for Mr. Lister, or via GitHub issues on the public redirect repository.
